# Dry Street candidate

This bank is an alternative for comparison with Original, which remains the default.
Its source brief is dry, close natural Foley: rounded shoe contacts, small hard
urethane skateboard wheels, dry powerslide friction and short wooden-deck/wheel
landings. The prompt asks for less ringing without removing skateboard identity.

The committed shortlist contains 53 distinct mono 48 kHz PCM16 WAVs (2,985,244
bytes): four contacts for each of seven foot pools, one rolling loop per eight
skate surfaces, two landings per skate surface, and one powerslide loop. Contacts
are separate animation-plant events, not baked walking sequences. Rolling and skid
assets retain the production extractor's crossfades. No timing thresholds, PCM
bytes or levels were changed to fill a missing role.

Every variant records its actual raw take checksum, model/weight revisions, prompt,
seed, sampler, processor checksum and exact source interval/crossfade. The shortlist
prefers different raw takes before additional contacts from the same take; the
wood contacts come from four separate repair takes. Unselected and rejected takes
remain authoring evidence outside the source library.

The game's existing material table maps Tatami to `barefoot`, gravel/dirt to
`dirt_gravel`, and wet mud to `mud_water`. Those three role mappings are explicit;
no generic surface was silently substituted. Existing hoof, other skate cues and
unrelated audio families inherit Original.

Source checks and complete coverage do not establish subjective listening quality.
Softness, recognizably skateboard acoustics, fatigue and the actual game mix remain
listening acceptance. This increment must not be represented as accepted audible,
menu, network or packaged-game behavior before the corresponding native evidence.
